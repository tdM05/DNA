If any multitude whatsoever of numbers is set out continuously in a double proportion, (starting) from a unit, until the whole sum added together becomes prime, and the sum multiplied into the last (number) makes some (number), then the (number so) created will be perfect. For let any multitude of numbers, $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, be set out (continuouly) in a double proportion, until the whole sum added together is made prime. And let $E$ be equal to the sum. And let $E$ make $FG$ (by) multiplying $D$. I say that $FG$ is a perfect (number).
